\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Traffic congestion in Dhaka, Bangladesh, creates severe challenges for millions of daily commuters due to narrow roads, mixed traffic patterns, and inefficient traffic management systems. This thesis presents an intelligent traffic management system that integrates Machine Learning and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies to address these urban transportation issues. The proposed system utilizes CCTV cameras and the YOLOv11 object detection model to automatically monitor and classify vehicles, pedestrians, and emergency vehicles in real-time, enabling dynamic traffic signal adjustments based on actual traffic conditions.

The research involved collecting and annotating 3,784 images from major Dhaka intersections, resulting in a dataset of 171,436 labeled objects across three categories: regular vehicles, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians. The YOLOv11 model was trained on this dataset and integrated with Arduino and Raspberry Pi microcontrollers to control physical traffic signals. The system incorporates dynamic signal timing based on traffic density, emergency vehicle prioritization, and fair lane management to prevent traffic starvation.

Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in traffic efficiency. The trained YOLOv11 model achieved 79% accuracy in vehicle detection, while the system reduced average vehicle waiting times by 33% and emergency vehicle response times by 56%. This cost-effective solution provides a scalable approach to traffic management that can be implemented in other developing urban areas facing similar congestion challenges.

\textbf{Keywords:} Traffic Management, YOLOv11, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, IoT Systems, Emergency Vehicle Prioritization, Dhaka Traffic
